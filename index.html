Everything you need to know before we start working together.

Ask Something Else
How long does a project typically take?+

Turnaround depends on the project size. A short reel takes 1–2 days. A YouTube video or brand film typically takes 3–7 days. Cinematic projects can take up to 2 weeks. Rush delivery is available on request.

What footage format do you accept?+

I accept all major formats — MP4, MOV, MXF, R3D, BRAW, ProRes, and more. You can send footage via Google Drive, WeTransfer, or any cloud platform. I'll handle the rest.

How many revisions are included?+

The Starter plan includes 1 revision, Professional includes 3, and Premium includes unlimited revisions. Additional revisions for Starter and Professional can be added at a small fee.

Do you provide music for videos?+

Yes! I can source royalty-free music from licensed libraries that fits your video's mood and tone. If you have specific music in mind, I'll work with that too.

What payment methods do you accept?+

I accept UPI, bank transfer, and PayPal. A 50% advance is required before starting, with the balance due upon delivery of the final file.

Can you work with international clients?+

Absolutely. I work with clients worldwide. All communication happens via email, WhatsApp, or video call, and delivery is fully digital. Time zone differences are never a problem.
